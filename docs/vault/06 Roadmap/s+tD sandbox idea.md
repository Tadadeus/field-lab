# s+tD dimensions sandbox (idea, not built)

A sandbox where you choose **s** space and **t** time dimensions.

- Signature vs labels: the metric signature (s, t) is what matters, not which axis is called "time".
- Light cone shape changes with t (t = 1 is a cone; t ≥ 2 has no ordinary causal cone).
- t ≥ 2 gives **ultrahyperbolic** equations: ill-posed as an initial value problem.
- Tegmark's (s, t) grid: which signatures allow predictable physics.
- Case 2+2: **unpredictable does not mean unstable**. Solutions can exist and be bounded; the initial data just does not determine them uniquely.
- Honest labelling per case: say "well-posed", "ill-posed", "not studied here".
