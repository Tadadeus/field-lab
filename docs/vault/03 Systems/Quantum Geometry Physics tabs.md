# Quantum, Geometry and Physics tabs (`qm`, `geo`, `ph`)

Extra structures layered on the 4D field labs. Each section has a reset button (↺).

- **Geometry (`geo`)**: Background spacetime · Generalized geometry on T ⊕ T* · Supersymmetry ℂ⁴|𝒩.
- **Quantum (`qm`)**: Non-commutative space [xᵘ, xᵛ] = iθᵘᵛ · Quantum group U_q(sl₂) · Interference and spinors.
- **Physics (`ph`)**: Constants of nature · Path integral · Strings · Solitons and instantons · Ambitwistors and scattering.

Many scenes in [[Scene catalog]] turn these on (e.g. the Ambitwistor n = 6 scattering demos in [[Lab Sedenion]]). Tab list: [[Settings and app popover]].
