# Guided tour

`TOURS` has 10 steps (`tourStart`, `tourGo`, `tourPos`). Each is `[selector(s), title, text]`; several selectors separated by `|` are highlighted together. Shown once, remembered in `bitwistor-tour`; can be replayed from App settings.

1. **Sets** (`#segTh`): Pick what to explore: twistors, Courant algebroids, octonions, su(3), Clifford algebra, qubits, sedenions, a lattice field lab, a particle universe, fluids, or laws you write yourself. Keys 1 – 9 and 0 switch sets too.
2. **Scenes** (`label[for=scene]|#scFav|.scfind|#scNext`): Every set has ready-made scenes in this list; ‹ › step through them. The search box (/) finds a scene in any set, and ☆ keeps favourites at the top.
3. **Play controls** (`#play`): Play or pause (Space), step one frame (→), run backwards, restart, undo and redo settings, save a picture, record a video, compare A / B (C), sound (M), shortcuts (?), app settings and full screen.
4. **3D view** (`#gl`): Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to recentre. Click to drop a probe that measures the field at that point; the bookmark button saves camera views (K).
5. **Timeline** (`#hist`): The run is recorded while it plays. Drag back to any earlier moment, press play to continue from there, or “live” to jump back to now. Keys , and . step through it.
6. **Side panels** (`.side`): The same state seen another way: components, charts or live measurements, depending on the set. CSV downloads the numbers behind a chart.
7. **Settings** (`.setp`): Tabs group the settings and the headers fold open and shut. ↺ on a header resets that section to the scene values, and the search box filters every tab. Hover or tap a slider name to read the physics and units behind it.
8. **Telemetry** (`#tiles`): Live readouts with a 12-second trace. The pin on a tile keeps up to 4 of them in a dock that stays on screen, even in other sets.
9. **Quality** (`#qBtn`): Pick how smooth the pictures are, from Potato (fastest) to Ultra (supersampled pixels and finer grids). Auto adapts to your device.
10. **App settings** (`#appSet`): Theme, text size, quality, sound, the glossary (G), an offline copy and this tour live here.
